Visual Identity Direction · v1

A wordmark, a mark, and a system built to sit beside Cartier and Bulgari — not to try to out-shout them.

Two names are live right now: Cassian, vetted and lower-risk, and "Taigerian," a flagged placeholder. Both are shown below so the choice can be made with the actual look in front of you, not just the word on a page.

02 · The Mark

An abstract facet, not a borrowed symbol

Rather than a literal emblem, the mark is built from the step-cut facet pattern of the stones themselves — reads as "gemstone" without leaning on any one culture's iconography, and works equally as a monogram seal, a hallmark stamp, or a favicon.
